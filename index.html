How We Got Here

Three honest lines about us

01

Born in finals week: We started out of a cramped student kitchen because library coffee tasted like pencil shavings and existential dread. We vowed to make coffee that actually sparks joy.

02

Radically ethical: Every single bean is directly traded with smallholder farming collectives in Colombia and Ethiopia. We pay 40% above fair-trade minimums and roast fresh weekly.

03

No coffee snobs: Want oat milk? Pistachio cold foam? Decaf with extra syrup? We will never judge. Bring your laptop, bring your dog, and sit as long as you need.
